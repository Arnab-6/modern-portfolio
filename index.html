MeetingShield — 100% On-Device Meeting Intelligence

MeetingShield is a privacy-first meeting intelligence system running Google Gemma 2 directly on your device. It analyzes audio, extracts critical decisions, assigns action items with owners, and detects consensus gaps across discussions without sending a single byte of audio or transcripts to closed cloud APIs.
